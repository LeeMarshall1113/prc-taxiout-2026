# CatBoost baseline v1 — 2026-09-04

Submitted as `jolly-lobster_v1.parquet`. First model, no tuning.

## Setup

CatBoost, RMSE loss, depth 8, lr 0.08, 6 threads. 28 features, 10 categorical.
Fit on months 2-6, 8-10, 12 (1,578,296 rows); early stopping on month 11
(162,332); Jan and Jul scored once (344,419) and tuned against never. Stopped at
iteration 1,057 of 3,000 by the overfitting detector. 15 minutes wall.

## Result

| split | RMSE |
|---|---|
| inner val (month 11) | 314.53s |
| **holdout (Jan + Jul 2025)** | **459.23s** |
| holdout, flight plan present (98.44%) | 278.4s |
| holdout, flight plan missing (1.56%) | 2,937.3s |
| holdout, worst 100 rows removed | 260.4s |
| holdout, 149 rows y>=7,200s removed | 285.2s |

The 1.56% of rows with no flight plan carry **63.8%** of squared error. The
worst 10 rows alone carry 53.9%.

## Two hypotheses killed

**"The field shrinks the flight-plan-less group toward the bulk and the leaders
do not."** False, at least for this model. CatBoost given `has_flight_plan`
reproduces that group's conditional mean almost exactly — 1,452s predicted
against 1,457s actual, versus 1,008s/1,005s for the bulk. It comes for free and
is not the lever between 320 and 277.

**"The model ignores the extreme rows."** Also false. For the 7 holdout rows
above 80,000s it predicts 19,929s on average — it knows they are dangerous. The
truth averages 86,895s, so it still eats them. Knowing a row is risky is not the
same as knowing it is a day-rollover error, and nothing in the features
distinguishes those.

## What the submission is really for

Our holdout is dominated by 16 rows. A single unpredicted 87,000s row costs
146.4s of RMSE by itself at n=344,841; two cost 348s of floor. The leaderboard's
best is 277.29, which is arithmetically impossible if the 2026 targets contain
even one such row. So either 2026 is clean or the organisers filtered.

We cannot see the ranking targets, so this submission is the experiment:

- score near **285** -> targets are clean, the bulk model is already near the
  leaders, and the entire competition is a bulk-modelling contest.
- score near **460** -> targets are not clean, and the standings are mostly a
  lottery over which corrupt rows each model happens to miss.

Either answer redirects the next two weeks, which is worth more than the board
position.
