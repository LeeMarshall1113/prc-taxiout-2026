# The tail is Rome — 2026-09-04

Baseline v1 scored **423.12** on the leaderboard (rank 10 of 18), against a
Jan+Jul 2025 holdout of 459.23 and a bulk-only 285.2. So the 2026 targets do
contain corrupt rows, but fewer than the 2025 holdout months. That killed the
clean/dirty dichotomy in `2026-09-04-baseline-v1.md` — it is neither.

It also forces a conclusion about the leaders. Our flight-plan-present holdout
RMSE is 278.4s; the leader's **whole** score, tail included, is 277.29. Their
total is below our bulk alone. They are not merely better at the bulk — they are
predicting the tail.

## Where the tail lives

Of the 41 rows in 2025 with taxi-out >= 40,000s, **38 are at LIRF (Rome
Fiumicino)**: 236.5 per million there against 8.3 at LFPG and 7.4 at LSZH, the
only other airports affected at all. This is an airport-specific data pipeline
fault, not a physical phenomenon and not spread noise.

Crossed with the flight-plan flag it isolates cleanly:

| group | rows | share | mean | median | sd | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| everything | 2,085,047 | 100% | 991s | 912s | 546s | 131,167s |
| LIRF, flight plan | 159,216 | 7.6% | 1,145s | 1,024s | 589s | 87,002s |
| **LIRF, NO flight plan** | **1,488** | **0.071%** | **6,531s** | 3,922s | 11,215s | 131,167s |
| non-LIRF, no flight plan | 20,982 | 1.01% | 1,019s | 912s | 1,196s | 87,341s |

**0.071% of rows hold 37.4% of the target's total variance.** And note the last
row: away from Rome, a missing flight plan is almost unremarkable — mean 1,019s
against a global 991s. The earlier reading that `has_flight_plan` is the key
feature was half right. The interaction is the feature.

## It is stable, so it can be bet on

Monthly conditional mean of the LIRF/no-plan group across 2025:

```
Jan 7446   Feb 9199   Mar 8328   Apr 7052   May 5571   Jun 6069
Jul 6064   Aug 6382   Sep 6377   Oct 4701   Nov 9375   Dec 7706
```

4,701s to 9,375s, never collapsing toward the global mean, present in all twelve
months. This is a persistent conditional mean, not one bad month leaking.

Our model predicts about 1,452s for the no-flight-plan group as a whole. RMSE
wants roughly 6,500s for the Rome part of it. On 1,488 rows that is worth on the
order of 18,000 MSE, or about 20s of RMSE — and probably more once the model
stops averaging Rome together with the other 21,000 harmless rows.

## To be clear about what this is

This is learning a conditional mean from the training data. It is not what the
ranking page warns about — their warning is aimed at probing the ranking process
itself to reverse-engineer labels, which is what a 542-submission sweep looks
like. Fitting `E[y | airport, flight-plan-present]` is ordinary modelling.
