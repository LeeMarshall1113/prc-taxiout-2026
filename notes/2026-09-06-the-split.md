# The split: a dedicated model for rows with no flight plan — 2026-09-06

The largest effect measured in this project by an order of magnitude.

## Result

Three folds, against the same baseline, one seed each:

| config | per-fold delta | mean | wins |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| split only, 800 iters | −76.32, −44.68, −38.50 | **−53.17** | **3/3** |
| 2500 iters only | +2.19, +1.69, −4.02 | −0.05 | 1/3 |
| **split + 2500 iters** | **−80.75, −49.41, −42.68** | **−57.61** | **3/3** |

What it does to the group it targets:

| fold | no-plan rows | global model | dedicated model |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| (1, 7) | 5,373 | 3,001.3 | **2,153.8** |
| (3, 9) | 3,547 | 2,153.9 | **1,337.8** |
| (5, 11) | 2,961 | 2,558.2 | **1,953.6** |

## Why it works

The global model's three most important features are `gap_aobt` (18.4%),
`gap_sched` (16.4%) and `gap_eobt` (11.5%) — 46% of total importance. **Two of
those three are null for every no-flight-plan row**, along with the operator,
market segment, wake category and flight type.

So on precisely the 1.5% of rows carrying ~61% of squared error, the model was
navigating with most of its instruments dead, falling back on whatever its
missing-value splits happened to do — while spending its capacity on the 98.5%
where those features exist. A model trained only on those rows, using only the
18 columns that are actually populated for them, uses stand, runway, time of day
and congestion properly instead.

In hindsight this was visible from the first day's feature-importance table. I
read `has_flight_plan` as *a feature the model should have* and never asked the
next question: what happens to the rest of the feature vector when that flag is
zero.

## Why this is not the 2024 trap

A 2024 team lost 2,066 → 2,276 by training per-aircraft-type models and picking
each by validation RMSE. That is multiple comparisons dressed as modelling.

Here nothing is selected. The group is defined by which columns exist rather
than by any score, the split is fixed before any measurement, both halves are
scored together on folds not used for training, and the routing key
(`AOBT_3_flt` is null) is present in `ranking.parquet` at the same rate.
Audited: the 18 features are 100% populated on the 5,290 ranking rows the
dedicated model would score, and neither blanked column appears among them.

## The iterations result is worth keeping too

2500 iterations alone wins 1 fold of 3 and is worthless on its own — but it
improved the bulk on every fold (282→276, 220→215, 214→209) and the tail noise
simply swamped it. Once the split removes that noise, the same change is worth
about 4.4s on all three folds. A real effect can be invisible behind a larger
one; that is an argument for fixing the dominant term first and re-testing what
you rejected.
